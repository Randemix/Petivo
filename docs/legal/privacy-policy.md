# Petivo Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026  
**Contact:** lysohmara.maxim@gmail.com  
**Developer and data controller:** Maxim Lysokhmara, individual developer, Ukraine  

Petivo (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a pet care organizer for **iPhone and iPad** (iOS / iPadOS 17+). This policy explains what data the app processes, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

---

## Summary

- Petivo keeps your pet data **on your device** and, if iCloud is on, in your **private iCloud** so it syncs between your own Apple devices.
- The optional **Community** (forum) publishes the content you choose to post. It is **public** and visible to other Petivo users.
- We **do not sell** your data, we have **no advertising**, **no analytics** and **no third-party SDKs**. Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, StoreKit, Maps) is the only third party involved.
- Community has **zero tolerance for objectionable content**: you can **report** posts, **block** users, and we remove violating content.
- You can **export** your data and **delete** your account from Settings.
- Petivo is **not a medical service** and does not provide veterinary advice.

---

## Data we process

### Data you enter

- Pet profiles (name, species, breed, photos, dates, notes)
- Health records (vaccinations, medications and confirmed doses, symptoms, vet visits, weight, documents)
- Care reminders, feeding plans and food stock, activity and walk logs
- Service contacts (vets, groomers). Addresses you enter are turned into map positions with Apple Maps.
- Optional owner profile and emergency contact details

### Data from your device (with permission)

- **Notifications** — local reminders for care, medications, feeding and vet visits, plus push notifications for forum threads you follow
- **Camera / Photos** — pet photos and medical document images
- **Location (when in use)** — nearby vets and groomers on the map, and an optional city label for forum posts. Only the city name is stored for the forum; your coordinates are never uploaded.
- **Face ID / Passcode** — optional app lock and medical records lock
- **Live Activities and widgets** — walk timer and pet summaries on the Lock Screen and Home Screen. Widget data is shared with the app through an App Group on your device.
- **Emergency card and QR code** — the emergency card, its PDF and QR code can include your pet's name, chip number, your phone number, allergies and current medications. The QR code is plain text: anyone who scans it can read it, so share it only deliberately.

You can deny permissions; related features will be limited.

### Community (forum) content

If you use the optional Community, what you submit is stored in Apple’s **public CloudKit database** and is visible to other users:

- Threads and comments you post, including text and any photos you attach
- Ratings and reviews of places (vets, groomers) you write, with your display name, and the places they refer to (name and map position)
- Your public display name, your avatar, your city (if you add one), whether you have Petivo Pro, and the pet details you choose to attach (name, species, breed)
- Your Apple user identifier, stored with each post so that you can edit and delete it and so that moderators can act on reports
- “Helpful” votes and reports you submit
- Moderation records (reports, blocks, bans) needed to keep the forum safe

Reading the forum is available without an account. **Posting, commenting, reporting and voting require Sign in with Apple.** Do not post sensitive personal information in the Community. Community content can be read by us and by moderators.

Photos you attach to Community posts are re-encoded before upload, so location metadata (EXIF) is not published. You can edit or delete your own threads and comments at any time.

### Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive a stable user identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. The identifier is used for private iCloud sync, family sharing, and forum identity. Guest mode does not require an account.

### Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the **Apple App Store** through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details.

## How we use data

- To provide app features (reminders, automatic feeding and walk tracking, health index, weight trends, exports, emergency card)
- To send notifications you configure
- To keep widgets up to date
- To operate and moderate the Community

We do **not** use your pet health data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

---

## Where data is stored

- **On your device:** database (SwiftData) in the app sandbox, photos and documents in the app documents folder, preferences in UserDefaults and an App Group for widgets. Data on your device is protected by iOS data protection.
- **Private iCloud sync (Apple CloudKit):** your personal pet data is synced through your own iCloud account in Apple’s **private CloudKit database**. It is tied to your Apple ID and is not readable by us or other users.
- **Family sharing (optional):** if you invite family members, the shared pet data — including health records, documents and photos — is shared through Apple CloudKit sharing with the people you invite. Your own owner profile is not shared.
- **Community:** content you post is stored in Apple’s **public CloudKit database** (see above).

iCloud and CloudKit data is processed by **Apple**, which may store it on servers in different countries under Apple’s privacy terms. You can turn off iCloud for Petivo in iOS Settings.

## Data sharing

We do **not** sell or rent your personal data. Data is shared only when **you** choose to:

- Post in the **Community** (becomes public)
- Invite **family members** to shared pet data
- Export a PDF or data file, or share the emergency card or lost-pet poster
- Contact support by email

We may disclose information if required by law.

## Community safety and moderation

- **Reporting:** any post or comment can be reported from the “…” menu.
- **Blocking:** you can block a user so you no longer see their content. The block list is stored on your device.
- **Filtering:** posts containing abusive language are rejected before they are published.
- **Moderation:** reported content is reviewed, violating content is removed, and abusive accounts can be banned. We aim to act on reports within **72 hours**.
- **Appeals:** if your content was removed or you were banned and you think it was a mistake, email **lysohmara.maxim@gmail.com**.

To report content or a user directly, email **lysohmara.maxim@gmail.com**.

## Retention and deletion

- Data on your device and in your private iCloud stays until you delete it or delete your account.
- **Deleting your account** (Settings → Account) removes your pets, records, photos, reminders, settings, and local recovery copies from this device, and the deletions are synced to your private iCloud while you are online. Family sharing is left or transferred. Your Community profile is removed and your forum posts are **anonymized**: your display name is replaced, but the text of posts and replies other people have responded to may remain. Your place reviews and their “helpful” votes are deleted; reactions you gave in the forum may remain without your name.
- You can delete your own threads and comments at any time from the Community.
- Moderation records (reports, blocks, bans) are kept as long as needed to keep the Community safe.
- Deleting the app does not by itself delete data stored in iCloud or the public Community; use the options above first.

## Your rights and choices

In **Settings** you can export your data, delete your account, turn off notification categories, disable Face ID locks, and manage subscriptions in the App Store.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to **lysohmara.maxim@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU or UK, we process your data to provide the service you asked for (performing the contract), to keep the Community safe (legitimate interests), and, for optional device permissions, with your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings. In Settings you can also **import** a data file previously exported from Petivo; import only adds records that are not already on the device.

## Children

Petivo is not directed at children under 13, and the Community is not for minors. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it. In some countries the minimum age is higher (for example 16 in parts of the EU).

## Security

We use iOS security features (sandbox, data protection, optional Face ID lock) and Apple’s encrypted CloudKit transport. No method of storage is 100% secure; keep your device passcode enabled.

## International users

Petivo is available worldwide. Data synced through iCloud or posted to the Community may be processed on Apple servers outside your country.
